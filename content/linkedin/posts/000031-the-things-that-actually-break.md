Most reliability books describe what should happen.

This chapter describes what actually happens.

The gap kills systems.

Ten hard truths:
1. Identity failures kill everything
2. Latency is failure
3. Partial failure is the norm
4. Monitoring fails when you need it
5. Humans slow down recovery
6. Failover is harder than you think
7. Data corruption is worse than downtime
8. Dependency chains are longer than expected
9. Capacity is a failure domain
10. Reliability work gets cut first

None of these are theoretical. All of them will happen to you.

What is the one hard truth your organization has already learned the hard way?
