# Editorial Policy

## Core principles

- Be useful before being impressive.
- Prefer practical patterns over abstract theory.
- Use measurable examples where possible.
- Mark uncertainty and assumptions explicitly.

## Voice quality standard

- Posts must read like a human operator wrote them, not a template.
- Avoid repetitive transition stems across sections (for example repeated "This is" / "That is").
- Remove AI-filler language and replace with concrete actor, action, and consequence.
- Vary paragraph length and sentence openings to prevent robotic cadence.

## Approval gate

Every post requires manual approval before publish for:

- Factual correctness
- Confidentiality and customer sensitivity
- Tone and positioning alignment
- Repetition and voice quality alignment

## Prohibited content

- Confidential customer details
- Internal-only roadmap information
- Sensitive incident details that can identify parties
