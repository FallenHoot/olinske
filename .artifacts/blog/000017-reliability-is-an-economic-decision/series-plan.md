# 10 Days of Reliability

## Operating model

This series exists to distribute the ideas in the book while also improving the book.

- 000017 is the living manuscript.
- 0018 onward are serialized public posts.
- Each post should stand on its own.
- Each post should also produce material that can be merged back into 000017.

The series is the outward-facing publication plan. The book is the inward-facing canonical source.

## Recommended numbering model

- 000017 = living book source
- 000018 to 000027 = 10 Days of Reliability
- 000028+ = optional bonus chapters, case studies, appendix pieces, or field tools

If the content naturally needs 11 chapters, use 000028 without forcing a weaker 10-post structure. The distribution theme matters more than perfect numbering symmetry.

## Editorial rule for each serialized post

Every post should include:

1. one sharp doctrine statement
2. one reusable model or table
3. one uncomfortable operational truth
4. one practical move for this quarter

## Merge-back rule

After each post is drafted or published:

1. extract the strongest original framing
2. add the best table, checklist, or model to 000017 if it strengthens the manuscript
3. remove duplicate or weaker wording from 000017 where necessary
4. keep 000017 as the strongest canonical version of the idea

## Why this model works

It avoids choosing between a book and a post series.

You get both:

- a living master document with doctrinal depth
- a public 10-day sequence that increases reach and pressure-tests the thinking
