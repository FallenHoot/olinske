## Survival-guide tone guidance

The book should feel like a field manual for organizations trying to stay alive through outages, budget cuts, reorgs, and platform drift.

Use this tone:

- direct
- practical
- unsentimental
- measurable
- biased toward action

Avoid this tone:

- inspirational
- academic
- vendor-marketing language
- hyperscale-only assumptions

## Why this direction is stronger

As a post, 000017 risks becoming too dense for most readers.

As a book foundation, the density becomes an advantage because it gives you a doctrine, a vocabulary, and multiple chapter spines that can be expanded cleanly.
