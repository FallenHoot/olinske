A company raises prices to fund a reliability upgrade. Fifty percent of customers leave.

Was that the right call?

Chapter 12 of the Reliability Survival Guide introduces the break-even churn threshold: a single formula that tells you whether a price increase recovers more value than the churn it triggers.

The answer is often no. And there is a better model.

What is your current margin per seat, and what would a reliability upgrade actually cost to run?
