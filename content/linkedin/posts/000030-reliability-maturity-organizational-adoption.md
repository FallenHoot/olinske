Your team will resist a reliability system.

Not because reliability is unimportant. Because the system feels like bureaucracy added on top of their actual work.

Chapter 13 closes the Reliability Survival Guide with the adoption path that works: four phases over 18 months, sequenced against organizational incentives, designed to handle resistance.

Start with one SLI. Not a reorganization. Not a new team. Just: can we measure what matters?

If you can answer that honestly, the rest is detail.

What is your first critical journey that needs measurement?
